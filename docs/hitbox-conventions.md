# Vehicle hitbox direction

The user selected the T-72A v2 patch's render-triangle approach on 2026-10-13. Future vehicle work must use the same geometry source for visible surfaces and contact queries. The current reference is [the T-72A interface](t72a-real-hitbox-interface-2026-10-13.md) and `client/web/src/collision/meshHitbox.js`.

1. Capture the actual uploaded Float32 triangle positions. Preserve apertures, gaps, rims and separate instances; do not replace them with convex hulls or filled boxes. AABB/BVH tests only select candidates. A triangle miss stays a miss.
2. Pose body, turret, trunnion, recoil, MG, hatch and running-gear instances from the accepted simulation sample. Detached or physically removed parts update collision membership. Camera/X-ray visibility changes do not remove armor. Frozen replay samples remain independent of subsequent poses and detonation animation.
3. Give geometry a content hash and pair it with stable part/instance/triangle identifiers and pose/geometry revisions. Temporary local mesh counters are not a cross-process registration format. Native and WASM consume verified build-time catalogs, not arbitrary client-supplied meshes.
4. Separate surface roles and registered armor/module bindings from geometric evidence. Rubber, glass, canvas and fittings do not inherit nearby main-armor thickness. Caller bindings retain explicit values, including null; missing values may use declared defaults. Semantic callbacks cannot overwrite measured contact points, normals or geometry identifiers.
5. First contact, subsequent penetration, scattering and re-intersection use the same posed surface scene. Do not select the first point from triangles and then silently resolve penetration against the old shell planes. Pair material-region entry/exit boundaries so two sides of one armor wall do not double-charge full thickness. Model-wall distances and candidate labels alone do not establish measured historical thickness.
6. Test real apertures and finite segments, world/nonuniform transforms, independent joints and recoil, side-specific damage/removal, pose ownership, repair/respawn, native/WASM parity, and immutable replay contact identity. Declare approximations in moving-target time of impact, projectile shape and internal anatomy until their actual owners are connected.

Current status: T-72A v2 exposes a working model-surface API. Formal combat still uses legacy plates/boxes; the native registration and all live damage paths remain outstanding. The original submitted results are preserved separately from the integration's own verification.
