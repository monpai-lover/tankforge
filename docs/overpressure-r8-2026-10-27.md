# R8 blast-route audit and limited model

Date: 2026-10-27. Source workspace: `D:/TankForge-workspaces/r8-20261027/worktree/online-source`.
The R7 binary, bundle and validation artifacts are immutable inputs. This document describes a game approximation, not historical ammunition certification or a human-injury prediction tool.

## Concrete missing paths

The accepted local path is `handoff/bundle/app.js.html` → `splashNear` → `Combat.nearBurst` → the registered `combat_surface_near_burst` WASM dispatch → `tg_combat::near_burst::evaluate`. The source client under `client/web/src` is not the canonical frontend for this build.

Before R8, `near_burst::evaluate` admitted HE/APHE/HESH and rejected HEAT/HEAT-FS as an unavailable charge model. That removed their exterior casing effects even though their shaped-charge jet belongs to the independent direct-contact path. It also applied no primary external crew blast, even when the posed triangle scene could demonstrate an unobstructed line to a registered crew surface. Fragments could still hit crew; they are a separate mechanism and cannot substitute for the missing primary component.

The canonical local frontend additionally contains `heStandoffBreach`: it clamps the real explosion to a vehicle AABB, selects the thinnest plate center within 1.5 m and calls `heInternalBurst`. That helper relocates explosions toward an invented hull center and up to five crew stations. `heContactEffects` uses a similar relocation and suppresses HEAT exterior casing/blast unless its approximate breach threshold beats a nearby plate. These are separate caller problems; passing a core unit test does not remove them.

The native `frontline_sim::splash` uses the older `tg_combat::splash` interface, carrying only explosive mass. Lobby `Mutation::Burst` likewise drops the accepted projectile's casing/type. `he_effects::contact` has its own guessed nearest-plate and synthetic internal-burst logic. Native ground combat currently has no registered `CombatScene` on these paths. This limitation must remain explicit until actual posed triangle registration is connected there; legacy planes/boxes are not equivalent evidence.

## R8 bounded change

- An already initiated HEAT/HEAT-FS near burst may create exterior pressure/casing fragments. It does not create a second jet or infer a new internal detonation. Accepted authority still determines whether a projectile actually detonated.
- External crew primary blast is allowed only when the registered posed scene finds a real crew-triangle contact without intervening armor, an unknown physical surface, or another physical body. The global `open_top` flag does not open an enclosed driver's station.
- Each crew station is evaluated independently. A real modeled opening remains open; no hull box, convex fill, nearest plate center, or arbitrary crew kill sphere is evidence of a gas path.
- Direct-contact exterior primary pressure and initiated-near primary pressure share the same exposure function. A recorded direct primary cannot bypass a driver's screen through the old global height rule, or suppress a genuinely exposed registered crew triangle merely because the vehicle's global `open_top` is false. Direct exterior burst records keep the measured impact while the visibility query uses a separate one-millimetre exterior offset.
- Casing fragments retain their existing per-piece masses, energy/penetration accounting, deterministic seeded sampling and separate report provenance. Numerical representative weight does not increase a fragment's ability to penetrate armor.
- Existing charge/distance response constants remain gameplay estimates. Large BMPT-34 charges retain larger candidate ranges and stronger exposure/fragment effects, subject to actual path controls. The TT-250 definition is **250 kg total mass with 125 kg TNT filler**, not 250 kg explosive.
- Unknown surfaces remain barriers. Native calls without a registered scene retain a specifically labeled `legacy_declared_plane_pressure_v1` approximation: `open_top` admits candidate stations, but each ray must encounter that station before any posed declared armor, unknown surface or other physical body. Closed legacy vehicles have no invented opening. This preserves open-station gameplay until native registered scenes exist; it is explicitly not triangle parity. `unknown_pressure_boundary` remains truthful: there is no complete pressure-cell, opening-area, reflection, diffraction or structural-deformation model.
- Every affected report retains the actual explosion position and one exterior burst; visibility alone does not transport a charge into a compartment.
- `include_pressure` is optional and defaults to true in WASM. A contact with a trusted preexisting exterior burst uses the casing-only response: no repeated primary damage and no duplicate detonation record. Internal APHE does not select this suppression. Direct contact queries use a one-millimetre exterior numeric offset from the measured impact, rather than the prior 0.15 m offset or invented internal points.
- Trusted raw projectile mass and raw filler reach casing evaluation. Caller TNT-equivalence inflation must not silently subtract extra mass from the casing. Current pressure/fragment energy coefficients remain an explicit uncalibrated estimate; this change does not claim independent peak-pressure and impulse equivalence.
- `explosive_type` is preserved as identity; the current near-burst pressure and casing-energy laws still consume raw filler mass without an independent filler-energy or pressure-equivalence coefficient. Equal raw TNT and PETN filler therefore currently receive the same exterior response. Retaining raw casing mass does not establish energetic equivalence across filler types.

## Caller and performance boundary

`handoff/tools/patch_overpressure_r8.py` writes an explicitly named candidate; the integration owner alone applies it to the canonical bundle. The patch removes the guessed standoff/internal-charge paths, retains each accepted exterior near burst and keeps HEAT identity. Bomb aftermath skips a ground/building splash that the shell already resolved; a direct store contact retains one adjacent-target burst with the direct target excluded. A store disappearing without contact evidence cannot create a new ground blast.

Queued contacts use the accepted combat report's local impact, rather than a collision helper's possibly approximate running-gear point. Queue entries retain actor battle/entity/life, combat/key/vehicle binding and a range-revival epoch; a changed binding or life cancels the old followup instead of damaging a fresh vehicle.

Registered surface actors always keep their triangle route, including 35 mm autocannon contacts. The existing 40 mm legacy budget policy applies only where no actor surface registration exists. Queue ordering, the six-millisecond admission budget and its existing at-least-one-item rule remain; this budget cannot interrupt one expensive triangle evaluation. Consequently the registered high-rate path still requires actual browser frame-time measurement. The Node regression executes a registered 35 mm contact and an unregistered legacy control, so this accuracy/performance boundary cannot silently move.

Run `node handoff/tools/test_overpressure_r8.mjs <candidate-bundle-path>` for the actual extracted production functions. This is the focused existing caller batch for small HE, HEAT, raw filler/casing identity, single primary, large-charge synthetic-route rejection, near misses, actual bomb aftermath and online authority guards. It is a behavioral boundary test, not a performance benchmark.

Contact queue results are accepted followup state advances after direct-shot replay admission. Their primary/fragments/bursts are not retroactively inserted into an already frozen single-shot replay. Native reports preserve the new casing segments and accepted state; complete replay presentation of every local contact followup remains a distinct integration limitation. Do not label such followups a complete shot replay merely because their live damage is correct.

The actual player `selfMissileHit` and `selfAirRoundHit` paths now use one immediate `playerExteriorContact` followup with the same raw shell/primary policy. The measured local impact and remembered missile impact pose are retained. If the followup supplies an actual admitted replay, it is shown as `contact_exterior_followup`; the helper never fabricates a source to obtain one. If the followup changes damage without such evidence, the stale initial replay is removed and the explicit `contact_exterior_followup_not_in_single_replay` limitation is recorded. Existing direct APHE/solid-shot reports do not enter this helper.

## Recommended causal-replay followup

The next replay step is to admit one exterior detonation child event from the already accepted discharge/contact, including the measured impact pose, projectile identity, charge identity, original time and real surface revision. Queue processing must append the actual pressure/casing report to that event after its accepted state commit, rather than mutating an already frozen shot report or inventing a new source flight. The viewer can then show the direct jet/spall/internal APHE and exterior blast/casing as distinct recorded phases. This requires changes in the accepted replay owner and validation schema; a presentation-only merge is insufficient.

Direct core HESH still uses its existing spall/overpressure approximation; R8 exterior routing does not certify a real gas breach from a HESH scab. Full multi-layer HE breach, coupled structural response, dynamic opening formation and diffraction remain unimplemented. APHE retains its tested native delayed-fuse internal detonation path; unarmed kinetic filler is not promoted to contact HE by these adapters.

## Primary sources checked 2026-10-27

1. [DoD BIRCO, What is Blast Injury?](https://blastinjuryresearch.health.mil/index.cfm/blast_injury_101/what_is_blast_injury). Distinguishes direct pressure injury from secondary fragment/debris injury. This supports preserving separate `blast` and `burst` report mechanisms, not a particular game health constant.
2. [DoD BIRCO, The Science of Blast](https://blastinjuryresearch.health.mil/index.cfm/blast_injury_101/science_of_blast). Pressure peak, duration, medium, distance and enclosure affect response; vehicle interiors create complex interactions. A direct unobstructed component is only a bounded approximation. Occlusion here does not assert that real diffraction, structural shock and vehicle acceleration have zero effect.
3. [UNODA, IATG 01.80:2015, Amendment 1 (2016), Formulae for ammunition management](https://s3.amazonaws.com/unoda-web/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/IATG-01.80-Formulae-for-Ammunition-Management-V.2.01.pdf). Sections 5–7 separate scaled distance, pressure, impulse and TNT equivalence; section 8 treats fragmentation and its assumptions separately. Section 11's outdoor risk guidance has applicability limits and is not a universal armored-compartment lethality rule. We do not transplant isolated physiological thresholds or outdoor fatality equations into crew health.

No copied source formulas, calibration tables, historical armor measurements or third-party implementation code are introduced by this change. The current mass-bin distributions, empirical penetration, blast radii and health response are labeled estimated game behavior.
