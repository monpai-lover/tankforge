## R snapshot reuse and player-specific capture

Existing offensive flow is `updateShots` / `updateBullets` / own missile hit → `combatHit` → `onCombatHit` → `hitcam.show(camTarget(e), ...)`. `combatHit` (`game/enemies.js:283`) takes `captureImpactPose(e)` **before** core damage, associates it with the returned report using `rememberImpactPose`, then applies destruction and `Enemy.pose`. It adds `r.shot` for replay direction. `Enemy.pose` at 147 changes dead turret yaw by .35 and dead gun pitch to .08; `onCombatHit` at 2123 can start a turret toss. R preserves the original pose instead of that death pose.

`camTarget` (`main.js:493`) assumes an Enemy's model/body/bundle/loadout and lazy interior. Player protection/test examples at 489 and 1074 already demonstrate a target descriptor containing `G.model`, `G.veh.body`, `G.loadout` name/pivot, armor, crew, dimensions, and `G.interior`. A focused player descriptor should also include `G.damageLabels`, folded armor from the actual bound bundle, and the original model/interior identities so replacement cannot redirect a lazy getter to a new vehicle.

Do not call `captureImpactPose(G)` directly. Its current enemy contract (`game/replayPose.js:15`) uses `e.turretYaws/e.turretYaw`, one `gunPitch[i]` per turret, and numeric `e.fold`; the player stores `G.T[ti].yaw`, separate `G.T[ti].guns[gi].pitch/recoil/roll`, `G.MG[i].aim`, and `G.fold.pose`. A player capture adapter must preserve every gun independently, parent-relative turret yaw, recoil/barrel roll, MG articulation, fold, and launcher eligibility. The player render synchronizes these fields later at `main.js:4000–4038`, after missile damage; live model locals can be from the previous draw when core damage is resolved. Read the accepted player command/body sample directly, without advancing recoil/physics or temporarily writing live nodes.

The body frame must use `body.worldPoint([0,0,0])` and basis vectors, not the center-of-mass position as hull origin. Capture before replacing state, applying death effects, toggling model pose, selecting, or resetting. For final exact geometry, use the same accepted pose sample as X/B's query—including current wheel/shoe/fold/crew/payload state—rather than combining a current body with previous-frame running-gear instance matrices. This preparation does not certify current player accepted-pose parity.

R's `snapshotReplayTarget` at `replayPose.js:50` already provides a detached graph, cloned mutable node fields, independent instanced meshes/matrices, retained non-instanced meshes, cloned interior maps, frozen launcher eligibility, and transactional/idempotent disposal. `HitCam.show` snapshots only after the invalid-report/MG guards; replacement acquires the new snapshot before releasing the old one (`hitcam.js:67–104`). Hide/expiry releases it (106–115). Rendering excludes the original subtree and uses the frozen target during both approach and X-ray (`hitcam.js:191`). Preserve these guarantees, including optical sight frames that omit the live player's exterior.

Use at most one displayed replay snapshot and a bounded authority causal record per actor/cause. Keep source/report/compact pose data while alive; acquire full GPU snapshot only for an accepted displayed replay. If a delayed cause must preserve an old contact pose/model after a vehicle is removed, its bounded ownership and release must be explicit. Do not accumulate a snapshot for every hit, retain per-frame graphs, share mutable track instance buffers, or retain lazy closures that follow a later `G.model/G.interior`.

R's current compact capture stores body/joints/launchers; running-gear meshes, crew locals, and other dynamic graph fields are cloned at `show`. That synchronous direct-hit design does not preserve those fields across seconds of driving before a delayed fire death. Prefer retaining the corresponding complete accepted X/B pose sample as bounded CPU data and constructing the detached dynamic geometry from that sample at presentation. If reconstruction is not yet available, an explicitly bounded causal snapshot retained at the actual ignition/source transition is an interim alternative: replace/release it when that cause is superseded, extinguished, expired, or its actor lifecycle ends. This must not become full GPU acquisition on every hit, and it still needs the authority cause identity before the retained record can be presented as causal.

The delayed mechanism has two distinct times: the initiating shot's contact pose and the victim's final pre-death pose. Old report hull coordinates belong to the first. Do not re-anchor the initiating shot's path to a victim that moved before burning out. ## Source identity and causal receipt gaps

`ServerMsg::Damage` (`lobby.rs:308`) has only `target`, `from`, `result`, `plate`, `hp`, `killed`, and optional `report`. `tg_combat::Report` (`crates/combat/src/lib.rs:774`) contains outcome, shell kind/caliber, impact/path/fragments/bursts/events, state/caps, turret yaw, and P1 mechanism metadata. It does **not** serialize the outer shooter/target identity, shell catalog ID, weapon instance, shot sequence/missile ID, shot seed, speed/distance, launch, target hull frame, authority actor epoch, or accepted pose revision. Its mechanism-local event IDs are not globally unique shot/death IDs. JS's `rep.shot` added by `combatHit` is not in the Rust Report sent to the victim.

The received Fire message does have shooter, sequence, origin, direction, and shell (`lobby.rs:305`), but `net.on('fire')` at `main.js:1938` drops `m.seq`. More fundamentally, Damage has no matching sequence, so selecting “latest Fire from this shooter” or matching caliber cannot identify the lethal round when shots overlap, an autocannon fires a burst, or multiple independent weapons share ammunition. Do not correlate by latest outgoing `G.lastHit/G.lastCombat`, current sight gun, nearest projectile, name, or vehicle spec ID. Two entities may use the same vehicle.

The upcoming B authoritative actor epoch/accepted pose and P5 trusted fire owner should supply one compatible source envelope for replay. Needed fields, without creating a parallel new protocol in this preparation:

- Authority match/actor context and victim incarnation/epoch; target and shooter entity IDs separate from spec IDs; accepted contact/death tick and pose revision.
- Stable shot/launch ID (accepted cannon/MG sequence with shooter context or missile ID), stable damage/death event ID, and causal parent mechanism event ID. Preserve original shooter through reflected/residual/fragment legs; a reflector is not automatically the shooter.
- Weapon kind, owned weapon instance, projectile catalog ID and, for missiles, launch definition plus warhead. Use the exact authority seed and source event time.
- Trusted launch origin/direction, flight samples or authoritative terminal line, actual speed/travel distance/time, the report in its declared hull coordinate frame, and the matching accepted victim body/articulation sample.
- Direct or delayed death cause, with the initiating report/pose reference and the authoritative terminal fire/ammo event when applicable. Missing legacy fields remain explicitly unavailable, never guessed from current loadout.

For an interim direct route, the actual `m.report` can be shown without resolving damage again. Freezing the current player before receipt application is a useful presentation fallback, but it is the **receipt-time local pose**, not proof of the historical pose used by the shooter/server. The current server trusts parts of WireShot speed/dist/yaw and the defender's submitted state; P5/X/B must close that gap before claiming exact accepted flight/pose. Do not turn this fallback into a completion claim.

Delayed cause is currently insufficient: `Player.last_attacker` (`lobby.rs:388`) is overwritten by `apply_shot` at 1429, including ordinary accepted hits, and `advance_combat` credits that ID. It stores no report or ignition event. `start_fire` (`combat/src/lib.rs:1118`) replaces `fire_at` and extends the fire, but emits the `fire` string only if fire was previously absent. Merely caching the most recent report with a `fire` event also cannot distinguish a changed fire origin during an ongoing fire. P5 causal retention must follow the actual source of the active damage mechanism using identity-linked events/state, not just the scoring attacker or a toast string.

Immediate ammo detonation inside a direct lethal report is part of that exact shot. A later burn, delayed rack detonation, APHE charge event, or fragment continuation must carry its actual parent/source chain and terminal cause. If the current `report:None` fire receipt cannot be correlated authoritatively, retain the true death label and replay-unavailable status until the P5 causal receipt exists. Never supply a fictitious new enemy shell or label a stale unrelated hit as the lethal event.

## Lifecycle and precedence considerations

- Trigger once on the actual offline alive-to-destroyed report transition, or once on authority self `Damage.killed` online. Online predicted impact and `Status.caps.destroyed` alone do not authorize a lethal shot replay. A dead snapshot confirms death without identifying its cause.
- Keep “dead state applied” and “victim replay presented” distinct under the same authoritative life/event context. A valid same-life lethal report after `markDead(null)` must still be eligible once; repeated Damage/Status/Snap/reconnect receipts must not restart it. Late previous-life packets must not overwrite fresh state or bind to the newly selected vehicle. Use B/P5's accepted context, not a second ad-hoc spawn counter.
- Victim lethal replay must take priority over an existing offensive replay, including a lethal MG round. Current `HitCam.show(..., mg=true)` suppresses MG during a shell replay at 69; passing the victim through that unchanged guard could silently lose its only death replay. Conversely, an outgoing shell still in flight can call `onCombatHit` after death and replace the victim. A focused replay-purpose/priority policy should preserve existing offensive shell/MG behavior when no victim replay owns the view.
- `markDead` schedules deploy at 3 s (1750), but a full current HitCam replay lasts 5.25 s. `netStep` opens deploy after that timer (1774), and `openDeploy` hides the replay (586). Avoid automatic truncation of the accepted victim replay: coordinate automatic deploy with replay expiry/skip while preserving the user's immediate R/deploy choice and server respawn delay. This is lifecycle integration, not a reference-derived timing/style claim.
- User R/Backspace/reset, chosen deploy, Tab/garage, battle end, disconnect, selection, remote removal, and renderer failure must cancel or safely finish according to existing control semantics. Explicit navigation cannot be blocked by replay. `HitCam` currently has no separate replay-close input handler; do not invent one from the unseen reference.
- `select` at 654 disposes the player's live model/interior; `respawned` at 2009 calls it, while offline reset at 1219 calls `resetPose/combatInit`. `clearEnemies`, garage, and deploy hide HitCam; `removeRemote` disposes a target without hiding, which R leases already support. Reuse this lease model and clear causal eligibility at the proper owner lifecycle boundary. A still-displayed detached snapshot must never adopt a replacement vehicle.
- Preserve ordinary live death/toss/burning effects. Do not freeze or rewind the main world to obtain replay. Only the replay copy is frozen.
- J hold abandonment (`main.js:2705`) is an explicit local destroyed state or online `respawn` request. The authority publishes `scuttle`, `from == target`, and no report (1085). Flooding at `wade` (1460) currently stalls the engine rather than supplying an enemy kill; crash/stuck recovery has no incoming shot receipt. These cases must not fabricate an enemy shell. Future actual self/reflected damage can replay its real causal record, with truthful self/team attribution; do not blanket-drop all `from == target` and thereby erase a future legitimate self-hit.

## Minimal approach options

1. **Pure direct-report routing first:** add a small victim target/capture adapter and one death-transition routing seam; consume actual offline `selfMissileHit` reports and actual online lethal `m.report`. Reuse R snapshots and the existing camera. This establishes presentation behavior with explicit legacy identity/pose limitations; it cannot complete exact online correlation or delayed fire replay by itself.
2. **P4/P5 integrated route (recommended):** keep the same thin presentation adapter, teach P4 an incoming replay purpose/priority and lifecycle policy, and have P5 publish/retain the trusted causal source using B's accepted actor/pose context. Handle direct and delayed deaths through that single source owner; P4 only interprets the report. Camera styling stays pending the attachment. Stage reviews around the actual existing plans and preserve all B/W/X/P2–P5 requirements.
3. **Separate victim HitCam and independent history/protocol:** would duplicate snapshots, cleanup and view ownership, and compete with B/P5 epochs. It offers no source-truth advantage and is not recommended.

Option 2 is the completion target. Option 1 is a bounded staged implementation inside P4, with limitations recorded and final P5 acceptance still pending. Do not add offline cannon/MG AI, camera styling, a parallel actor epoch owner, or another combat damage computation to this work.
