## Implementable common propagation architecture

The current dependency direction is penetration → damage → combat. Design and sim-slice depend on penetration/damage/replay; they do not normally depend on combat. Sim-slice has combat/design dev dependencies for parity. Placing the entire shared scheduler inside combat and then calling it from damage would introduce a cycle.

Keep family state transitions/profile logic in `tg_penetration::impact`. Put the graphics-free bounded propagation scheduler and individual-versus-weighted response in `tg_damage` (a focused module is suitable). Make `crates/combat/src/projectile_effects.rs` a geometry/state/report adapter for `Work`; design and sim-slice provide adapters to the same scheduler. When X exposes trusted pure geometry from combat, design/sim-slice may depend on that query owner without combat depending back on either consumer. This preserves the existing acyclic direction.

The scheduler consumes an immutable accepted pose/query adapter, canonical target effects adapter, trusted projectile profile, one shot budget, and continuation states. A continuation includes current finite position/segment, complete `ImpactState`, source/parent, canonical representative identity/weight, traversal/crossing cursor, and contact count. Geometry supplies nearest semantic contacts with entry/exit/normal, layer/material resistance, stable binding/entity identity and accepted pose revision. An AABB can filter candidates but never authorize final X damage.

For each scheduled continuation:

1. Query the next contact within its finite segment; advance actual elapsed time at the current speed. For an unchanged direction, a cached contact list is only valid for the unchanged accepted pose and segment. After reflection, bending or split, discard it and query from outgoing state.
2. For a declared solid crossing, invoke one shared family collision transition. Its ledger allocates target work, carrier energy, finite pieces, armour debris, deformation/fracture and unresolved loss. Generate each allocation once. Perforation/allowed outgoing side must be distinct from the visual/mechanism label `fractured`; a fractured body is not automatically inside.
3. For module/crew contact, obtain resistance from pre-commit health/occupancy, consume only local deposited energy, carry the remaining state, and commit the canonical weighted target response once. A killed crew member and health-zero visible metal do not imply identical geometry removal rules.
4. Enqueue bounded outgoing bodies/pieces. Primary and fragments use the same contact loop. Pieces from an unpenetrated collision start outside; an epsilon cannot teleport them beyond the intact plate. Target armour spall has separate local target mass, not projectile mass. Emit segment/event snapshots and terminal disposition.
5. When a continuation leaves a scene, return its residual state for a world owner; do not silently discard it or call scene-local output cross-vehicle authority. P5 supplies world/entity ordering and shot dedupe.

Retain P1's work cursor for partial same-layer progress and extend it with cumulative family allocation progress. Cap consumption, mass removal and deformation must consume incremental progress just as work does. Piece transfer invalidating a traversal must be an explicit finished crossing/branch, not a hidden cursor reset halfway through one physical layer. Profile response uses the fixed entry and accumulated state; split/unsplit same-layer updates must agree.

Charge-bearing state is a singleton: one actual reservoir owner, one charge ID, one fuse state. A fracture cannot distribute filler across several representative charged rays. If a charged ray is duplicated as two halfweights, congruent canonicalization must still recover one owner. Unknown survival gets an explicit estimated failed/unresolved disposition. Keep P3 chemical/fuse completion visibly pending; the current legacy APHE burst calls in combat and clamped design burst at `trace.rs:855–868` must not bypass the new surviving owner or recreate full casing/filler after P2 fracture. If pending P3 cannot detonate from a survivor safely, report unresolved rather than regenerate charge.

## Physical properties, representative weighting, and deterministic commits

Each representative stores individual physical `m/e/area/v` and an independent weight/multiplicity. Choose the physical distribution from the source/profile first; then choose ray count and weights. `sum(weight*m)` and `sum(weight*e)` equal the debited source budgets. Changing sampling resolution must not resize the individual fragment automatically.

Individual collision/penetration uses individual energy, mass and authored area. Weighted budgets and local deposition use `weight * individual_term`. With a nonlinear damage response, compute the individual local-deposition response then weight its contribution; do not feed aggregate representative energy into `sqrt(E)` or `E^.6`. Exact duplicate copies retain identical physical properties/direction/start time and split only weight.

Canonicalize congruent samples before budget admission, target-health clamps, secondary effects/RNG, and report event generation. The congruence key uses stable source parent plus semantic crossing/binding, individual physical state, exact canonical trajectory and start time, and inherited charge identity. It excludes vector position in the input sample list and arbitrary duplicate sample ID. Sum weights in a deterministic order; serialize one canonical group. Different trajectories cannot be merged merely because they hit the same module. No approximate angular tolerance should silently merge separate physical rays.

For each canonical contact group, snapshot absorption inputs, evaluate physical transition once, compute weighted local response, apply one health clamp, and make at most one secondary event decision. Derive stable physics/secondary substreams from trusted shot/source/crossing/target IDs, rather than relying on the number/order of sample draws in `Work.rng`. Full twin invariance includes health, caps, fire/ammo flags, newly-destroyed lists, event order/IDs, ledgers and residual continuations, not merely total joules. Stable source IDs cannot depend on source iteration order. P5 will provide the globally unique shot/entity namespace; seed alone is not uniqueness.

Resampling genuinely different directions only has bounded statistical convergence; exact equality is required only for congruent duplication and identical input/seed. FX randomness and display quality remain outside the physics scheduler. The current damage formulas are engineering response, not physiological calibration.

## Budgets and crossing stability

Use one shot-wide mutable budget shared across primary/piece branches and adapter calls: at most 64 representative physical rays, 256 total emitted/processed segments, and 16 semantic collisions per continuation. Define counting consistently at the common scheduler; no caller can reset counters per plate or family. Canonical duplicate groups consume one representative slot so a split into halfweights cannot alter truncation. Secondary piece generation consumes the same global counters.

Reject nonfinite mass/energy/area/weight/velocity before enqueue. Stable crossing identity and declared topology distinguish shared-edge duplicates, overlapping pieces of one declared layer, real exit/re-entry, and distinct coincident layers. Use minimum forward progress and bounded collision count as numerical protection, not triangle inflation or a fake hole closure. X finite segment/contact cursors must be preserved; no two-metre backshift and infinite full-vehicle raycast.

On exhaustion, record reason, source/parent, position/time, remaining state, and weighted unresolved mass/energy. Transfer that residual to a terminal unresolved disposition or parked unresolved continuation; do not both keep it active and count it as consumed loss. Do not assign the remainder to a nearby crew member. Track escaped energy separately from unresolved computation. Shot closure counts initial input, terminal residuals and actual cumulative dissipation; adding every collision `input_j` would count the same carried energy repeatedly.
