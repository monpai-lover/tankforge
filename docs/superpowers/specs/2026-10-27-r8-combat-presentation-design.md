# R8 combat presentation and equipment

The user requests reliable overlapping ballistic replays, prompt target switching, better overpressure, improved terrain/water shading informed by Shadertoy, and missing historical aircraft stores. R7 remains an immutable comparison and fallback. All implementation is isolated in the R8 worktree.

## Replay

Retain the installed supplied V3 renderer, accepted evidence, original target snapshot, first-round camera director and victim replay priority. Subsequent rounds overlap in the current view with independent monotone presentation clocks. Recorded body traversal, fragmentation and APHE detonation each receive observable presentation time; no physical path is invented. Bound traces per frame fairly across active events, reserving terminal evidence. Retire by each event's own presentation time. A finished offensive playback gives way to the next queued target after its evidence tail, without waiting for the optional same-target hold. New shots may create a fresh session after a previous session is finished. Deduplication also records appended rounds.

## Pressure

Resolve one initiated charge at its actual accepted location. Treat HEAT casing blast and fragments separately from its directional jet. Scene-backed local pressure requires a visible station or actual opening and respects intervening armor and unknown geometry. Keep the large BMPT charge definition. Native legacy exposure must remain an explicitly named declared-plate approximation until native triangle registration exists. Do not claim calibrated human blast injury or sealed-hull radius kills.

## Rendering

Keep a clear realistic appearance. Reuse existing procedural terrain scales for slope tint and bounded close detail; filter unresolved detail. Use independently authored analytic filtered water gradients. Preserve replay material roles, geometry, water levels, sky, shadows and fog. Do not copy Shadertoy code without suitable rights, introduce fullscreen raymarching, or invent shoreline depth.

## Equipment

Add verified presets through the existing compatible source station layouts. Preserve original presets, IDs, vehicle physics, guns and fuel. Record exact variant and evidence; distinguish confirmed carriage, already-custom-configurable equipment, and uncertain date/station claims. No arbitrary compatibility bypass.

## Verification and delivery

Regression tests must reproduce baseline failures before repairs. Exercise sustained fire, dense simultaneous fragments, delayed APHE, target changes, duplicate resend, victim priority, resource disposal and pressure shielding. Compile real WebGL shaders where the available environment permits; numeric checks alone do not establish visual quality or FPS. Validate native and WASM changes, profile synchronization and self-contained HTML, then provide a separate R8 client and Linux amd64 update with an honest limits report.
